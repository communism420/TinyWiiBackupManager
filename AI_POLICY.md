# Contribution and AI Policy

TinyWiiBackupManagerPlus welcomes useful, safe changes that do not break existing functionality, regardless of how they were made: by a person, with AI assistance, or by an automated tool. What matters is the benefit and quality of the result.

All contributions follow the same standards:

- Explain the problem being solved or the benefit of the change.
- Preserve users' files and existing functionality.
- Include appropriate checks and clearly state any known limitations.
- Address bugs or regressions found during review.

This policy applies to TinyWiiBackupManagerPlus. The original TinyWiiBackupManager project sets its own contribution policy.
